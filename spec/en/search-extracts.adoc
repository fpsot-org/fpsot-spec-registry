= Search, extracts and credentials
include::_fpsot-terms.adoc[]

Search is a regulated choice in a register.
Québec opened the search of its enterprise register by the name of a person on a set date.
The Québec register of movable rights charges fees by search criterion.
A missed registration under an exact or a similar match can change who has priority.
This page therefore treats each search mode as data of the profile, and each result as a record.

An extract or a certificate is what a third party relies on.
It must say which register, which subject, which instant and which last filing it reflects.
It must carry a seal under the signed records specification, which already covers register extracts.

== Search modes

[[REG-080]]
*REG-080.* Each register MUST declare its search modes in its profile.
Each mode MUST give its keys, its matching rule and the views that may use it.
A mode that the law opens on a date MUST give that date, under REG-014.
A register MUST NOT offer a search that its profile does not declare.

[[REG-081]]
*REG-081.* The matching rule of a mode MUST be `exact`, `normalized` or `similar`.
A `normalized` mode MUST compare search keys under the normalization steps of the scheme, under REG-051.
A `similar` mode MUST state its algorithm and its threshold.

Two registers that search one name with two algorithms can give two answers.
A stated algorithm lets a court see why a search missed a registration.

== Search records

[[REG-082]]
*REG-082.* A register MUST keep a record of each search, in the class `SearchRecord` of `model/registry.yaml`.
The record MUST give the mode, the keys, the instant and the highest sequence that the result reflects.
A register that certifies a result MUST issue it as an extract under REG-084.
The record MUST name that extract.

The personal property register of British Columbia keeps its search results as records of their own.
The last filing in the record tells a late filing apart from a search that missed one.

[[REG-083]]
*REG-083.* A search on personal information MUST use `POST` on the `searches` sub-resource of its collection, under API-049.
Each result MUST respect the visibility of REG-070 for the view of the caller.

A name or an address in a query string ends up in server logs and in browser history.

== Extracts and certificates

[[REG-084]]
*REG-084.* An extract or a certificate MUST be a record of the class `Extract` of `model/registry.yaml`.
It MUST state the register, the subject, the valid instant, the record instant and the last filing that it reflects.
A public view of a closed register states no subject and no last filing, under REG-012.
It MUST also state the view that it applies, the access policy behind it and each omission with its reason.
Each value MUST equal the replay of REG-042 for those instants.
The register MUST seal and log each extract under the signed records specification, at the seal profile of its register.

The fixture holds an extract for an enforcement officer and a public view of the closed register.
The check of the fixture rebuilds each value of the extract from the filings.

[[REG-085]]
*REG-085.* The layout of an extract SHOULD be data of the profile, apart from the record of REG-084.
The legal weight of an extract MUST NOT appear in a rule.
A profile MAY describe it in a note.

The EU Company Certificate of link:https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2025/25/oj/eng[Directive 2025/25 of the European Union] is a target worth naming.
It must apply from 31 July 2028, and other member states must accept it without an apostille.
Whether a Québec statement of information or a French Kbis proves anything in law is a question for counsel.

== Verifiable credentials

[[REG-086]]
*REG-086.* A register SHOULD also issue each entitlement as a verifiable credential under the link:https://www.w3.org/TR/2025/REC-vc-data-model-2.0-20250515/[Verifiable Credentials Data Model v2.0].
The status of the credential SHOULD follow the lifecycle filings, through a link:https://www.w3.org/TR/2025/REC-vc-bitstring-status-list-20250515/[Bitstring Status List v1.0].
A register MAY issue an extract as a verifiable credential too.
This version sets no wallet protocol.

Wallets are out of scope in the first version of the identity specification, and in this version too.
A credential with `validFrom`, `validUntil` and a status list works with the wallets that may come later.
Whether a closed register may issue a public credential is a question for counsel.

== Business registers

[[REG-087]]
*REG-087.* A domain pack for business registers SHOULD export the link:https://standard.openownership.org/en/0.4.0/standard/reference.html[Beneficial Ownership Data Standard 0.4].
It SHOULD also map its fields to the link:https://semiceu.github.io/Core-Business-Vocabulary/releases/2.2.0/[Core Business Vocabulary 2.2.0] of SEMIC.
The party stays a `Party` of DMO-040.

The Core Business Vocabulary gives a legal name, an identifier, an address, a type and a status, but no history.
The filings of this specification supply that history.
BODS splits statements from records, which mirrors the split between filings and derived state.
