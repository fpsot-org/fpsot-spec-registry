= Packs, events and conformance
include::_fpsot-terms.adoc[]

One core engine can serve many domains, and one domain can serve many jurisdictions.
A domain pack holds what a domain needs, such as the filing types of pleasure craft licensing.
A jurisdiction profile holds what a law sets, such as the five-year term.
This page sets the pack, the events of a register, its interfaces and the replay tests that prove it.

== Domain packs

[[REG-100]]
*REG-100.* A domain pack MUST hold these parts.

* A LinkML schema that imports `model/registry.yaml` and defines the asset classes of the domain, under DMO-010 and DMO-055.
* A file of the class `DomainPack` with its asset kinds, its identifier schemes and the catalog of its filing types.
* The lifecycle of each entitlement class, its payment and review statuses, its party roles and its evidence types.
* The default visibility and opposability of the fields that it defines.
* Replay fixtures with expected snapshots, under REG-105.

The pleasure craft pack holds `model/pleasure-craft.yaml` and `packs/pleasure-craft.yaml`, with its fixtures in `examples/pleasure-craft/`.
Drones will make a good second pack, once the research has read their official fee and validity.

[[REG-101]]
*REG-101.* Each pack MUST have its own version number under Semantic Versioning 2.0.0.
A pack schema MUST NOT redefine a class of `model/registry.yaml` or of the Party model.
A pack MUST NOT hold a value that a law sets, under REG-015.

== Events

[[REG-102]]
*REG-102.* A register MUST emit a business event under OBS-070 each time a filing reaches a final status.
It MUST emit another one each time a completed filing changes rows.
An event that it sends to another service MUST be a CloudEvents event under API-100 and API-101.
The types are `org.fpsot.registry.filing.recorded` and `org.fpsot.registry.state.changed`.
Their schemas are in `registry/events/`, under API-103.

The second event names each row that the filing opened, ended or left destroyed.
A consumer can then refresh its own copy without reading the filing.

[[REG-103]]
*REG-103.* An event of a register MUST NOT hold a party id, a name or an address.
It MUST NOT hold an amount or the value of an identifier either.
It holds only the ids of filings and rows, codes and instants.

A license number or a hull serial number leads to a person as surely as a name.
The pleasure craft register is closed, and its events must not open it.

== Interfaces

[[REG-104]]
*REG-104.* An interface of a register MUST follow the RESTful API specification.
It MUST create a filing with `POST`, with an `Idempotency-Key` under API-060.
It MUST NOT offer an operation that changes the content of a filing or deletes it.
A withdrawal MUST be a change of the processing status of REG-024 before completion, never a deletion.
The provider MUST test the interface against its published description, under TST-040.

A base path such as `/registry` MAY prefix the paths, under API-019.

== Replay fixtures

[[REG-105]]
*REG-105.* The conformance suite of a pack MUST hold replay fixtures, under TST-044.
The fixtures MUST hold ordered filings, the light chain and the expected snapshots of REG-042.
They MUST also hold scenarios in English and in French, under TST-020 to TST-036.
Each scenario MUST state its dates and name the filing types that it uses.
The suite MUST also test each event against the schema of its type, under TST-043.

A replay test feeds filings in and compares the snapshots that come out.
It is the strongest test of a register whose state comes from events.
It needs no access to the code.

[[REG-106]]
*REG-106.* Fixtures, examples and tests MUST use synthetic data only, under TST-050.
Their parties MUST be fictional, and their addresses MUST use domains reserved for documentation.
Their identifiers MUST NOT match a real license, hull or order.

The fixture numbers start with `EX`, and its order points to `example.org`.

== GovStack adapter

[[REG-107]]
*REG-107.* A register MAY offer an adapter profile for link:https://registries.govstack.global/[GovStack Digital Registries 3.0-alpha].
Such an adapter MUST map each GovStack requirement that it claims to a rule of this specification.

GovStack is generic about schemas and is still at an alpha version.
Its rules on read logging and logical deletion match REG-076 and REG-044.
An adapter gives buyers a familiar checklist without letting a generic schema drive the core.

== Replacement

[[REG-108]]
*REG-108.* A core engine that replaces another one MUST pass the same replay fixtures, under TST-045.
It MUST give the same snapshots and verify the same chain from the same filings.

A register can then change engines while the law stays the same.
Both engines give the same state from the same filings.
