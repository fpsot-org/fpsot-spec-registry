= Questions for counsel
include::_fpsot-terms.adoc[]

This page is informative.
It sets no rule, and it is not legal advice.
It lists the legal questions that this specification raises but cannot answer.
A lawyer must answer each one before {abbr-fpsot-first} or a body that keeps a register relies on it.

LégisQuébec, CanLII, Légifrance and the ISO store refused automated readers.
The research therefore read no Québec statute and no French code.
An article number marked _to be verified_ comes from a secondary source or a government page.
It does not come from the law itself.

== Legal effect and evidence

. *Legal effect.* For each register in scope, what does registration do in law?
It may identify an asset for the administration, give notice or opposability, or create or prove title.
Is any Canadian, Québec or French register in scope a source of title?
Under REG-011, no profile of this release may declare `title` until counsel names such a register.

. *Weight of an extract.* What weight in law do these documents carry?
They are the statement of the Québec enterprise register and the certified statement of the RDPRM.
They also include a certificate under the Canada Business Corporations Act, believed to be section 263, _to be verified_.
The last ones are a Transport Canada certificate, the French Kbis and an extract of the French business register.
REG-085 keeps that weight out of the rules until counsel answers.

. *Priority and the time stamp.* When does priority run under the Civil Code of Québec?
The articles at stake are 2934, 2969 and 2980, _to be verified_.
Does priority run from the entry in the register, or from the moment the application arrives?
Which instant must the system time-stamp: `submittedAt`, `completedAt` or another one?

== Publicity and privacy

[start=4]
. *Publicly available information.* Does a public Canadian or Québec register of personal information fall under an exclusion for public information?
The provisions at stake are section 69 of the Privacy Act and section 55 of the Québec Access Act.
Both are _to be verified_.

. *Access to the French beneficial owner register.* Secondary sources report a decree numbered 2026-310, _to be verified_.
What are its criteria of legitimate interest?
Is the access certificate, reported as valid three years, an administrative act that a person can appeal?

. *Carve-outs for minors.* According to law firms, Bill C-42 carves out minors and incapable persons, _to be verified_.
Do those carve-outs apply on their own, or only on application?
Which other prescribed circumstances exist?

. *Register of disclosures.* The SAAQ discloses under listed articles of the Highway Safety Code, _to be verified_.
It publishes a register of those disclosures.
Must a register then log the article of law that allows each disclosure?

. *A credential from a closed register.* The pleasure craft license register is closed to the public.
Can it lawfully issue a verifiable credential that the holder shows to anyone?
REG-086 makes that credential a SHOULD, pending the answer.

. *Reads hidden from the data subject.* REG-077 lets a data subject see who read their data.
May a body hold back the reads of the police or of other bodies, and under which provision?

== Integrity, retention and destruction

[start=10]
. *Integrity of a sealed filing log.* How do the Québec rules on integrity and signatures apply here?
They sit in the Act to establish a legal framework for information technology.
Secondary sources point to sections 5 to 7 and 39, _to be verified_.
Does an append-only log of sealed filings meet them when it serves as the legal record?

. *Destruction and the chain.* In 2012, the Ending the Long-gun Registry Act, S.C. 2012, c. 6, ordered the government to destroy the long-gun records.
Does a deletion of the content and the salt, with a tombstone that keeps a hash, satisfy such an order?
Does the tombstone of REG-094 keep too much, or too little?
Must a register also destroy the corrections and later filings that repeat a targeted value, as REG-093 asks?
What must it do when it cannot destroy one of them?

. *Retention instruments.* Which instruments govern filings, and which govern audit events?
The candidates are the retention schedules under the Québec Archives Act and the disposition authorities of Library and Archives Canada.
In France, the candidate is the Code du patrimoine, all _to be verified_.
How long must a register keep the tombstone of a destroyed filing?

== Pleasure craft licenses

[start=13]
. *Transfer of the license number.* The impact analysis statement of SOR/2025-272 says that a renewal keeps the license number.
Does a transfer keep it too, as the fixture of this specification assumes?

. *Legal basis of each access policy.* The impact analysis statement names three kinds of users.
They are the enforcement officers, the search and rescue agencies and the Coast Guard.
Which provision allows each disclosure to them?
The `ca-federal` profile cites the Privacy Act for each one, _to be verified_.
