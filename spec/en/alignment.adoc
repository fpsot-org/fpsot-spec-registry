= Alignment with other standards
include::_fpsot-terms.adoc[]

This page is informative, and it sets no rule.
No published standard models a register of assets and entitlements across domains.
Each standard below fits one part, so this specification maps to them and claims conformance to none.
A mapping lets an adopter export to a known format.
It also answers a buyer who asks about a standard by name.

== Land Administration Domain Model

link:https://www.iso.org/standard/81263.html[ISO 19152-1:2024] is the closest model of a register.
Part 1 is generic, and link:https://www.iso.org/standard/81264.html[Part 2 of 2025] covers land registration.
ISO sells both parts, and the research read only a draft of Part 1.
The class names below are therefore _to be verified_.
The 2024 edition adds a real-world lifespan beside the system lifespan, which makes the model bitemporal.
This specification takes the concepts and leaves the spatial packages out.

[cols="2,2,3",options="header"]
|===
|LADM |This specification |Note
|Party |`Party` of DMO-040 |The data models specification owns it.
|Basic administrative unit |`Asset` |One subclass for each kind of asset, in the pack schema.
|Right |`OwnerGroup` or `Entitlement` |Ownership and entitlement stay apart, under REG-056.
|Restriction |`Encumbrance` |An optional family, under REG-059.
|Responsibility |A condition of an `Entitlement` |A duty that comes with a right.
|Administrative source |`Filing` |The legal act behind each change.
|Versioned object |History slots of REG-041 |`validFrom` and `validTo` for the world, `completedAt` for the system.
|===

== Beneficial Ownership Data Standard

link:https://standard.openownership.org/en/0.4.0/standard/reference.html[BODS 0.4] splits statements from records.
Each statement has its own id, and each record has a status of new, updated or closed.
That split mirrors the split between filings and derived state.

[cols="2,2,3",options="header"]
|===
|BODS 0.4 |This specification |Note
|Statement |`Filing` |A statement never changes, and a filing neither.
|Record and its status |Row version and its history slots |A closed record is a row with `validTo`.
|Person statement |`Person` of DMO-041 |
|Entity statement |`Organization` of DMO-042 |
|Relationship statement |`OwnerGroup`, or `PartyRelationship` of DMO-044 |Ownership of a share, or control.
|Subject exempt from disclosure |Omission reason `withheld-on-application` or `not-public-by-law` |REG-073.
|Information unknown to the publisher |Omission reason `unknown-to-register` |REG-073.
|===

== Core Business Vocabulary

The link:https://semiceu.github.io/Core-Business-Vocabulary/releases/2.2.0/[Core Business Vocabulary 2.2.0] of SEMIC describes a registered business.
It defines no event and no lifecycle, so the filings of this specification supply the history.

[cols="2,2,3",options="header"]
|===
|CBV 2.2.0 |This specification |Note
|Legal name |`OrganizationName` of DMO-042 |
|Legal identifier |`PartyIdentification` of DMO-046 |
|Registered address |`PostalAddress` of DMO-033 |
|Company type |`PartyClassification` of DMO-047 |
|Company status |The state of the registration, under REG-045 |Derived from the filings, never stored alone.
|===

== GovStack Digital Registries

link:https://registries.govstack.global/[GovStack Digital Registries 3.0-alpha], of June 2026, lets a register create schemas with a lifecycle state.
It generates operations to create, read, update and delete records.
It has no holders, assets or entitlements.
Some of its cross-cutting rules match this specification, and an adapter under REG-107 can claim them.

[cols="2,2,3",options="header"]
|===
|GovStack |This specification |Note
|Schema creation |Domain pack, under REG-100 |A pack is released data, not a schema made at run time.
|Update and delete of a record |A new filing, under REG-020 and REG-022 |The adapter turns each update into a filing.
|DRS-8, logging of each read of personal data |REG-076 |An audit event under OBS-042.
|DRS-37, the data owner sees who read their data |REG-077 |A SHOULD here.
|Logical record kept on deletion, unless a law requires hard deletion |REG-044 and REG-093 |The destruction filing is the lawful hard deletion.
|===

== Verifiable Credentials Data Model

The link:https://www.w3.org/TR/2025/REC-vc-data-model-2.0-20250515/[Verifiable Credentials Data Model v2.0] fits issued entitlements.
The link:https://www.w3.org/TR/2025/REC-vc-bitstring-status-list-20250515/[Bitstring Status List v1.0] carries their status.

[cols="2,2,3",options="header"]
|===
|VC 2.0 |This specification |Note
|Issuer |The body that keeps the register |
|Credential subject |The `Entitlement` and its parties |
|`validFrom` and `validUntil` |`validity` of the `Entitlement` |Days in the time zone of the register.
|Credential status |Lifecycle filings, under REG-045 |A time-driven state needs no status change.
|Credential schema |The pack schema |
|Evidence |The filing and its evidence references |References only, under REG-092.
|===

== Once-Only Technical System

The European Once-Only Technical System maps evidence types to the requirements of each member state.
Its link:https://ec.europa.eu/digital-building-blocks/sites/display/OOTS/Technical+Design+Documents[technical design documents] describe that model.
The research saw a version 2.0 of February 2026, which is _to be verified_.
An extract type of a profile, under REG-085, is the natural place to name the matching evidence type.
