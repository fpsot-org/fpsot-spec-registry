= Assets, ownership, entitlements and encumbrances
include::_fpsot-terms.adoc[]

A register keeps four kinds of rows.
An {term-asset} is a thing that the register identifies, such as a vessel, a vehicle, a drone or a firearm.
Its {term-ownership} is a set of owner groups.
An {term-entitlement} is a right that the register grants to parties, such as a license, a permit or a registration.
An encumbrance weighs on an asset or on an entitlement.
A lien, a seizure, a stop order and a flag are encumbrances.

Ownership and entitlement stay apart.
The owner of an aircraft may differ from its registered operator.
A leased vehicle has an owner and a holder.
A firearm license belongs to a person, whatever firearms that person owns.

== Assets

[[REG-050]]
*REG-050.* Each asset MUST be a record of the class `Asset` of `model/registry.yaml`, or of a subclass of it.
Each domain pack MUST define one subclass for each kind of asset that it registers, in its own LinkML schema.
That schema MUST import `model/registry.yaml`, and each asset MUST name its kind in `assetKind`.

The pleasure craft pack defines `PleasureCraft` in `model/pleasure-craft.yaml`.
It holds the propulsion, the engine power and the length, which decide whether a craft needs a license.
A pack schema keeps typed fields, so a register needs no store of free keys and values.

== Identifiers

[[REG-051]]
*REG-051.* Each domain pack MUST declare each identifier scheme that it uses.
Each scheme MUST be a concept under VOC-001.
The declaration MUST give the issuer, the check digit method or none, the uniqueness scope and the normalization steps.
It MUST give the format when the pack read it at the source.
Each identifier MUST keep its value in the exact form of its issuer, under DAT-066.
It MUST also hold its search key, which the normalization steps give.

The schemes of this release are pack data in YAML.
A released scheme also needs its Turtle and JSON-LD files, under VOC-050.

Registration marks come back into use after a deregistration.
A uniqueness scope tells the register whether a new asset may take a mark that an old one held.
The pleasure craft pack declares the license number and the hull serial number, and does not yet state their formats.

[[REG-052]]
*REG-052.* A link between two assets MUST be a typed link, with a link type from the domain pack.
It MUST NOT be free text.

An engine on a hull and a trailer under a craft are links that a search may need to follow.

== Ownership

[[REG-053]]
*REG-053.* The ownership of an asset MUST be the set of its current owner groups.
Each group MUST be a record of the class `OwnerGroup`.
It MUST state its tenancy and the interest that it holds, as a fraction.
The tenancy MUST be a code of the profile, and the `core` profile gives these three by default.

* `sole`: one member, who holds the whole asset.
* `joint`: two or more members, who hold the whole asset together.
* `in-common`: a group that holds a fraction of the asset, beside other groups.

At each instant, the interests of the current groups of an asset MUST add up to exactly one.

Fractions as two integers never round.
Joint tenancy with survivorship comes from the common law.
Québec civil law knows undivided co-ownership, the _indivision_, instead.
The `ca-qc` profile therefore lists its own codes, _to be verified_.

[[REG-054]]
*REG-054.* A transfer MUST end the owner groups that it replaces and open new ones.
It MUST NOT change the members of a current group in place.

The manufactured home register of British Columbia closes owner groups on a transfer and opens new ones.
Its code sits in the link:https://github.com/bcgov/ppr[same repository] as the personal property register.
Each group then keeps the filing that opened it and the one that ended it.
Together they give the chain of title.

[[REG-055]]
*REG-055.* Each transfer filing type MUST list the causes that it accepts, such as `sale`, `death`, `court-order` and `operation-of-law`.
Each transfer filing MUST state its cause.
The evidence of a transfer, such as a bill of sale or a will, MUST be `restricted`.
A profile MAY widen it.

A transfer on death and a sale need different evidence and different checks.
The evidence often names people who are not parties to the register.

== Entitlements

[[REG-056]]
*REG-056.* An entitlement MUST NOT imply ownership, and an owner group MUST NOT imply an entitlement.
A filing that changes both MUST state each change apart, under REG-029.

[[REG-057]]
*REG-057.* Each entitlement MUST be a record of the class `Entitlement`.
It MUST hold its class, its number and its validity as dates.
It MUST also hold the role of each party, the covered assets, if any, and its conditions as codes.
The validity MUST follow DAT-023.
The register MUST turn the effective instant into a date in the time zone of its declaration.
The number MUST follow REG-051.

[[REG-058]]
*REG-058.* The lifecycle of each entitlement class MUST be data of its pack, under REG-045.
The term of a class MUST be data of the profile, with its source.
The profile MUST also list the filing types that start a new term.
The validity that a filing opens MUST equal the term in force.
The term counts from the date of the instant that its `countFrom` names.
When it names none, the term counts from the effective date.

Under section 105 of the amended Small Vessel Regulations, a license runs five years from its issue, transfer or renewal.
The `ca-federal` profile holds that rule as data, and the pack holds none of it.
A transfer may date from the sale or from the filing, _to be verified_.
The `countFrom` parameter therefore stays a choice of the profile.

== Encumbrances

[[REG-059]]
*REG-059.* A register SHOULD keep each lien, seizure, stop order or flag as a record of the class `Encumbrance`.
Each one SHOULD have a filing that opens it and filings that change or end it.
It SHOULD have a term when the law gives one.

The French vehicle register holds the liens, the objections and the suspensions of each vehicle.
The link:https://www.cnil.fr/fr/siv-systeme-dimmatriculation-des-vehicules[CNIL describes it] that way.
Québec keeps those rights in a separate register.
Transport Canada records no aircraft liens at all, under link:https://tc.canada.ca/en/aviation/reference-centre/advisory-circulars/advisory-circular-ac-no-202-002[Advisory Circular 202-002].
Only an optional family in the core, with a profile flag, covers all three.
The family maps to the restrictions of link:https://www.iso.org/standard/81263.html[ISO 19152-1], _to be verified_, as the page xref:alignment.adoc[Alignment with other standards] shows.

[[REG-060]]
*REG-060.* The rights over the assets of a register may live in another register.
The profile MUST then declare that other register.
It MUST also state the kinds of rights there and the identifier schemes that search them.
The register MUST NOT copy those rights as its own encumbrances.

The link:https://www.rdprm.gouv.qc.ca/fr/documents/publications/10_q_r_rdprm.pdf[Québec register of movable rights], the RDPRM, takes a search by the vehicle identification number.
The `ca-qc` profile declares it for the road vehicle register.
A copy of another body's rights would soon differ from the original and mislead a buyer.

== Parties

[[REG-061]]
*REG-061.* Each party of a filing or of a row MUST be a `Party` under DMO-040, named by its id.
A register MUST NOT keep its own records of persons or organizations.
The role of a party MUST be a code from the domain pack.

The data models specification owns the person, the organization, their names and their addresses.
A register that kept its own copy would hold two truths about one person.
