= FPSOT registry specification

Rules for software that keeps a register of assets, ownership and entitlements under a law, such as licenses, vehicle and vessel registrations, permits and business registers.
The specification standardizes the filing and what can be derived from it, and leaves everything that varies by law to jurisdiction profiles.
Each legal act is one immutable filing with three dates, three statuses and the authority of its filer, in a light chain that the FPSOT signed records specification seals and logs.
Current and past state come from replaying the filings, as of one instant and as recorded at another.
Visibility and opposability are separate data per field, and a lawful destruction deletes content and salt while the chain still verifies.
Version 0.1 is a draft.

== Implement this spec

Read the normative text in `spec/en/` or `spec/fr/`.
Each requirement has an ID from `REG-001` to `REG-108`.

* State your conformance classes, and never claim that a register meets a law (REG-001 to REG-006).
* Declare each register in a profile with its legal effect and publicity regime, and keep every legal value in the profile (REG-010 to REG-016). The profiles are data files in `profiles/`.
* Keep each legal act as an immutable filing with three dates, three statuses, its authority and its stated changes, sealed, chained and logged in a log series of the register's own (REG-020 to REG-034).
* Derive every view by replaying the filings, with history rows and bitemporal queries (REG-040 to REG-046).
* Model assets, owner groups, entitlements and encumbrances, with declared identifier schemes (REG-050 to REG-061). The pack data is in `packs/`.
* Set the visibility and opposability of each field, give a reason for each omission and log each read (REG-070 to REG-077).
* Declare search modes, record each search, and seal each extract (REG-080 to REG-087).
* Give each filing type a retention class, and destroy filings only through destruction filings that leave tombstones (REG-090 to REG-096).
* Write packs, emit events without personal values and test with replay fixtures (REG-100 to REG-108). The event schemas are in `registry/events/`.
* Run `bash examples/test-examples.sh` to check the example files, after `pip install --require-hashes -r examples/requirements.txt`.

== Adopt this spec

Read the guide in `docs/modules/en/pages/index.adoc` or `docs/modules/fr/pages/index.adoc`.
It takes one register through every step, with a synthetic pleasure craft license register as the example.
A request for proposals can require a core engine, a pack, a profile or a register operator to meet the conformance classes of REG-002, and to pass the replay fixtures of REG-105.
The comparison data in `jurisdictions/` shows how Canada, Québec and France handle the same topics.
The pages `alignment.adoc` and `questions-for-counsel.adoc` are informative, and the latter lists the legal questions that a lawyer must settle.
This specification is not legal advice.

== Layout

[cols="1,3"]
|===
| Folder | Content

| `spec/`
| Normative text, in English and French. `book.adoc` joins the pages into one book, published as PDF with each release.

| `docs/`
| Guides, in the Antora folder layout.

| `model/`
| The LinkML model of the registry, the schema of the pleasure craft pack, and unchanged copies of the data types and party models.

| `packs/`
| The data of each domain pack: asset kinds, identifier schemes, filing types, lifecycles and visibility defaults.

| `profiles/`
| The jurisdiction profiles, each released apart from the core rules.

| `registry/`
| The JSON Schemas of the data of each event type.

| `examples/`
| A synthetic pleasure craft license register, with its replay check, its test and scenarios in English and French.

| `skill/`
| The agent skill: a short summary that cites requirement IDs.

| `rules/`
| Machine-checkable rules.

| `diagrams/`
| PlantUML sources and their rendered SVG files.

| `jurisdictions/`
| Comparison data: how each government handles the same topics.
|===
