= Put a register under the registry specification
:page-type: how-to
include::_fpsot-terms.adoc[]

This guide takes a public body from one register to a conforming one.
Each step cites the rule that it meets.
It is not legal advice, and the page of questions for counsel lists the points that a lawyer must settle.
The files in `examples/pleasure-craft/` follow a synthetic register of pleasure craft licenses through every step.

== Declare the register

Find the law that establishes the register, and read what registration does in it.
Write the declaration in your profile, under REG-010.
Give the legal effect and the publicity regime, each with its source, under REG-011 and REG-012.
Mark each source that nobody read at its origin as `to-be-verified`, under REG-013.

Put every legal value in the profile, such as terms, deadlines and retention classes, under REG-015.
The file `profiles/ca-federal.yaml` declares the pleasure craft register, with the five-year term of section 105.

== Pick or write the pack

Use a published pack when one fits your domain, such as `packs/pleasure-craft.yaml`.
Otherwise, write a pack under REG-100.
It needs a LinkML schema for your assets, a catalog of filing types and a lifecycle for each entitlement class.
It also needs your identifier schemes and the default visibility of each field.
Keep the law out of the pack, under REG-101.

== Keep the filings

Write each change as a filing, and never as a direct update, under REG-020.
Give each filing its three instants, its three statuses and its authority, under REG-023 to REG-025.
Leave fees and eligibility to the decisions specification, under REG-026.
Keep the ids of the decision records in the filing.
At completion, state the rows that the filing opens and ends, under REG-029.

Seal and log each completed filing under the signed records specification, under REG-031.
Give it the next sequence number and the commitment that the filing before it got, under REG-030.
Log it in the log series of your register alone, under REG-031.

== Derive the state

Build every view by replaying the filings, under REG-040.
Answer each query as of a valid instant, as recorded at a record instant, under REG-042.
A table that you keep for speed must stay a projection that the replay can rebuild, under REG-043.

== Serve views and extracts

Give each view a visibility class and an access policy where it needs one, under REG-070 and REG-074.
Log each read of personal information as an audit event, under REG-076.
Record each search, and seal each extract that you issue, under REG-082 and REG-084.

== Correct and destroy

Correct a filing with a new filing that replaces it, under REG-027.
When it takes effect before a version already opened, restate that version, under REG-034.
When a law or an order requires a destruction, file a destruction filing, under REG-093.
Name every filing that holds a targeted value, corrections included.
Destroy the content and the salt together, and keep the tombstone, under REG-094.

== Check the result

Run the example check on a copy of the repository that holds your files under the same names.

[source,text]
----
pip install --require-hashes -r examples/requirements.txt
python3 examples/check-examples.py path/to/your/copy
----

A pass shows that the filings, the chain, the log and the snapshots hold together under the structural rules.
The same holds for your extracts, your events and your scenarios.
It does not test your engine, which the replay fixtures of REG-105 will do.
