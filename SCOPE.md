# Scope

This Working Group develops the FPSOT registry specification.

The specification covers software that keeps a register of assets, ownership and entitlements under a law, in any domain: the declaration of each register with its legal instrument, legal effect and publicity regime; the filing as an immutable legal event with its submission, effective and completion instants, its payment, review and processing statuses, the authority of its filer, corrections, orders and a light chain of sequence numbers and previous commitments; current and past state derived by replaying filings, with bitemporal queries and lifecycles; assets, owner groups, entitlements and encumbrances, with identifier schemes and typed links; field visibility, opposability, omission reasons and access policies; search modes, search records, sealed extracts and verifiable credentials for entitlements; retention classes, lawful destruction filings and tombstones; domain packs, jurisdiction profiles, events and replay fixtures. It includes a LinkML model, a pleasure craft pack, jurisdiction profiles, event schemas, example files and the scripts that check them.

It does not cover the intake of applications, fees, appointments and case management, which future FPSOT specifications will cover; the party, representation, data types, decisions, code lists, seals and the transparency log, audit events and their retention, and APIs, which other FPSOT specifications set; the choice of a database, an engine or a product; or legal advice.

Any changes of Scope are not retroactive.
